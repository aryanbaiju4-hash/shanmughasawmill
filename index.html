About Our Sawmill

With over 40+ years of experience, Shanmugha Sawmill provides premium quality wood processing and timber logistics. We specialize in turning logs into high-grade dimensional lumber, live-edge slabs, heavy-duty timber transport via crane service, and custom beams tailored to your construction needs.

Our Services

Custom Milling

Bring us your logs and we will saw them to your exact specifications using high-precision machinery.

Crane Service

Professional heavy-lifting crane operations for loading, unloading, and site transport of heavy timber and massive logs.

Rough-Sawn Lumber

High-quality hardwood and softwood dimensional lumber available in standard and custom sizes.

Live Edge Slabs

Beautiful, unique natural-edge slabs dried and ready for tables, countertops, and custom furniture.
